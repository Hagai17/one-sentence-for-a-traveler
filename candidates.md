# Candidates — One Sentence for a Traveler from 1800

Fourteen candidate sentences were drafted, counted exactly by script, and duelled pairwise on the judge's criteria in order; the round-1 winner was then beaten by a repaired variant of itself in the revision round, which put the pen in the traveler's own hand.

**Final winner (195 chars):** Picture a post office and a library joined in one small box upon your desk: every book and letter in the world lies inside it, and a letter you write to China is read there before your tea cools.

**Runner-up (185 chars):** One small book upon your desk holds every other book ever printed, answers any question you put to it, and carries your letter to the far side of the Earth and back while you read this.
